# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project. Be
constructive, assume good intent while addressing impact, and keep technical
criticism focused on the work rather than the person.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback
- respecting differing experience levels and viewpoints
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them
- protecting the privacy of contributors and users

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope And Enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove content
and contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through a
private GitHub channel when available. Do not include sensitive personal details
in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as
practical.
